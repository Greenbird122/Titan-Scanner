# Testimonial Request Templates

## Template 1: Direct Ask (After Delivering Report)

**Send after the client has reviewed the report:**

---

Hi [Name],

Glad the report was helpful! I have a quick favor to ask.

Would you be willing to write a short testimonial about the audit? Just 2-3 sentences about:
- What you expected vs what you got
- Whether the findings were accurate
- Whether you'd recommend the service

Here's a template if it helps:

> "Titan Security Lab did a security audit on [SITE]. They found [X] vulnerabilities including [Y critical ones]. Every finding had proof I could verify myself. The report was honest — they corrected false positives and told me what I was doing right. Fixed everything in [Z] hours. Highly recommend."

No pressure at all. But if you're happy with the work, a testimonial would really help me grow.

Thanks!

---

## Template 2: LinkedIn Request

---

Hi [Name],

Thanks for the testimonial! Would you mind posting it on LinkedIn? Tag me and I'll reshare it.

If you're not comfortable with that, no worries at all.

---

## Template 3: Google Review Request (If You Set Up a Google Business Profile)

---

Hi [Name],

Thanks for working with me! If you have a minute, would you mind leaving a review on Google?

[Insert Google review link]

Just a sentence or two about the audit experience. It really helps other businesses find me.

Thanks!

---

## What to Do With Testimonials

1. **Add to Fiverr gig** — in the description and FAQ
2. **Post on LinkedIn** — with the client's permission
3. **Add to portfolio** — as a "Client Feedback" section
4. **Use in outreach** — "Here's what a client said about our work"
5. **Create a wall of love** — a simple page with all testimonials
